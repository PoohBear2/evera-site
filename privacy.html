Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Evera is built so that what you share stays with you. This policy explains what the app stores, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

What Evera stores. The answers you give during setup, the stories and affirmations written for you, your journal entries, playlists, favorites, photos you add to your wall, and your settings are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to us and we cannot see them.

Sign in with Apple. If you choose to sign in, Apple gives the app an anonymous identifier for your account. It is stored on your device so the app can recognise you. We do not receive your Apple ID password.

Purchases. Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app asks the App Store whether your subscription is active so it can unlock your content.

Notifications. If you allow them, reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.

Microphone and photos. Evera only uses the microphone or your photo library when you ask it to, for example to add a photo to a moment or to record a voice sample. You can withdraw these permissions in your device settings.

Analytics and advertising. Evera does not include advertising or third-party tracking, and it does not sell or share your personal information.

Deleting your data. Profile, Settings, Delete account erases everything the app has stored on your device. Deleting the app does the same.

Children. Evera is not directed at young children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes. If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact. Questions about privacy can be sent to jzhou3379@gmail.com.
