Support

Need help with Evera? Email jzhou3379@gmail.com and we will reply within two business days.

Manage or cancel your subscription: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Evera.

Restore a purchase: on the Evera paywall or in Profile, Settings, tap Restore.

Delete your data: Profile, Settings, Delete account erases everything Evera has stored on your device.
